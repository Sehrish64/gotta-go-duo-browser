# Gotta Go Duo — Bathroom Rush 🚻

A fast, funny browser racing game: pick your chubby blob racer and **rush to the bathroom** before your panic meter maxes out!

## How to play
- **Tap / press-and-hold** RUN to sprint (hold = faster!)
- **JUMP** over hurdles
- **SPECIAL** — each character has a one-use skill
- Watch your **panic meter** — high panic triggers the desperate pee-dance!

## Characters
- **Pappu Panic** — Tiptoe Ninja: dash forward + lower panic
- **Captain Quack** — Waddle Dash: huge speed burst (35% HONK panic risk!)
- **Wizard Wobbly** — Cast Clench: big panic reduction
- **Mango Mike** — Juice Slide: dash + drop a peel that slows your rival

## Modes
- Solo vs computer AI
- Local 2-player (same screen)

## Tech
Single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies, mobile-friendly. Just open and play!
